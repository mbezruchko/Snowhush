# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mac, Windows and Linux users who spend long hours at their desk and want a little atmosphere on their screen without paying for it in battery, fan noise or distraction. They find the site through a link or a post, arrive curious, and decide within seconds whether the app feels right. The job: see what the snow looks like and how it behaves, trust that it is light, and download it.

## Product Purpose

Snowhush is a lightweight native macOS menu-bar app, with native Windows and Linux tray versions that do the same (Linux: KDE Plasma 6, Hyprland, Sway and niri on Wayland). Soft snow falls over the desktop, settles into drifts at the bottom of the screen, on the top edges of windows (following their rounded corners) and on the Dock, can be swept away with the cursor with real granular physics, slides off windows that are dragged or shaken, and drops in lumps that burst into powder when a window closes or minimizes. It stays out of screenshots and screen sharing and spends less on battery. In autumn, leaves can fall instead of snow. Options: Intensity (Off, Light, Steady, Heavy, Variable), Mode (Snow or Autumn Leaves), Accumulate, Melt Snow, Snow on Windows, Clear Snow, Behind Windows, Show in Screenshots, Open at Login. It uses about 6% of one CPU core and 65 MB of memory.

The site's one goal is the download: a .dmg, a Windows installer or a Linux AppImage from GitHub Releases, the visitor's own platform first. Success is a visitor who plays with the snow on the page and clicks Download.

## Brand Personality

Calm, atmospheric, lightweight. The app was asked for as "light, vibey, pleasant, creating a pleasant atmosphere": the site should feel the same, never loud, never busy. Copy is short, plain and warm; it describes what the snow does, not adjectives about it.

## Anti-references

- Holiday kitsch: Santa, trees, garlands, red and green.
- SaaS landing pages: gradients, grids of identical feature cards, hero metrics.
- Neon tech: dark backgrounds with acid accents and glow.
- Childish cartoon style: fluffy illustrations, mascots, toy colors.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell: the page itself snows, piles and can be swept, so the visitor feels the physics before reading about it.
- Lightness is a feature: the page stays as light as the app (no heavy frameworks or video), and says so plainly.
- Quiet confidence: few words, generous space, one clear action.
- Native, not novelty: it should read as a crafted Mac utility, not a seasonal toy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for all text. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: the snow stops or becomes a still scene, and nothing essential depends on animation. The interactive snow is decorative; all information is available as text, and the download works by keyboard.
