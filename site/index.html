What the snow does

Falls
Every flake keeps its own pace and sways in a slow breeze. Variable lets the snowfall rise and ease on its own over the day.
Settles
It piles up and slumps to a natural slope along the bottom of the screen, on any window with room above it, and on the Dock.
Moves
Push the cursor through a drift and it parts and sprays. Drag or shake a window and its snow slides off and lands below.
Drops
Close or minimize a window and its snow drops at once, in lumps that burst into powder where they land. Switch Spaces and the snow rides along with the window.
Melts
With Melt Snow on, the drift thaws slowly, so it never takes over your screen.
Hides
Screenshots and screen sharing show your desktop without it, unless you turn on Show in Screenshots.
Rests
About 6% of one processor core and 65 MB of memory, and less on battery or in Low Power Mode. It pauses while your screen sleeps, locks or is covered.
